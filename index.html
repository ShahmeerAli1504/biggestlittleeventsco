Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Why don’t you list prices?

No two events are the same. Your proposal is tailored to your venue layout, guest count and creative vision — so you only pay for exactly what your event needs.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Reno and serve Sparks, Lake Tahoe, Carson City and all of Northern Nevada. Our DJs also travel for destination events — just ask.

Do you provide the alcohol for bartending or VIP service?

No. All alcohol is securely provided by the client/venue host. We provide the certified, professional and fully insured hospitality staffing.

Can I book only a DJ, or only rentals?

Absolutely. Every service is available on its own — but bundling decor, DJ and hospitality means one contract, one point of contact and one synchronized setup.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and peak-season weekends, 3–6 months ahead is ideal. Weekend slots fill incredibly fast, so check your date as early as possible.

Do you handle setup and breakdown?

Yes — delivery, setup, styling and breakdown are part of every rental and décor proposal.
